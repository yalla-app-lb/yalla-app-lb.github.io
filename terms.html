2Places and information

  • Places come from open data and from users. Details like opening hours, prices or locations can be wrong or out of date: check before you go.

3Who can use Yalla

Yalla is not meant for children under 13. You must give a real email address and keep your password to yourself. One person, one account.

4Be decent

  • No offensive names or bios, no harassment, no impersonating anyone, no spam.
  • Only add places that really exist, and don't report places falsely.
  • Don't try to break the app, get into other people's data, or overload our servers.

Names are checked by a word filter, and an admin reviews problems. We may remove content, or suspend or delete an account, that breaks these rules.

5Your content

What you add (your profile, plans, places) stays yours. You let us store and show it inside the app so the app can work. How we handle your data is in the privacy policy.

6Changes and contact

If we change these terms, we update this page and the date at the top. Questions: yalla.app.lb@gmail.com.
