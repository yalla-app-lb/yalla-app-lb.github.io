An honest note

What's real, and what isn't yet.

Yalla is a student project. We'd rather tell you its limits up front.

The places are real

About 32,000 places from open data (Overture Maps, Foursquare's open places and Wikidata). Coverage is best in Beirut, Mount Lebanon, Keserwan-Jbeil and the North, and thinner elsewhere.

Some data is simulated

Bookable time slots at places are made up, and so is the social activity the models learn from.

No venue partnerships

We don't work with any venue and can't book anything for you. Yalla helps you and your friends decide; the rest is up to you.
