Yalla is a student project (CSC 490) by Yamen Morcel and Hadi Khawand. This page explains, in plain words, what the Yalla Android app stores about you, who else receives it, and how to have it deleted. "We" means the two of us.

The short version: we store what the app needs to work (your account, your free time, your friends and plans, your searches). We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't track your location in the background. Email us and we delete your account.

1What we store

Everything is stored with Supabase, in its Frankfurt (EU) data centre.

2Your location

  • The app asks for your location only while you use it, to show what's near you. It has no permission to read your location in the background.
  • When you search places while signed in, we save the spot you searched from (your phone's position, or the centre of the map) rounded to about 100 metres, together with that search. We keep it until you delete your account. We use it to train the ranker: it needs to know how far each place was.
  • Otherwise, your position is sent with a request only to answer it (for example "places near me" or "plans near me") and is not saved in our database. It does appear for a short time in our server's request logs.
  • The map images come from Stadia Maps, which sees which area of the map you're looking at.

3Who can see what

  • Other signed-in users can see your name, username, bio, photo and region.
  • Your free-time hours are never shown to anyone. The app only uses them to work out answers like "which friends are free Saturday?" or "the best time for this plan", and you choose which friends are included.
  • Plans are seen by their host and guests; friends-only plans by the host's friends; public plans by everyone.
  • Your searches, notifications and push address are visible to you only.
  • These rules are enforced by the database itself (row-level security), not just by the app.
  • Your profile photo can be opened by anyone who has its link.
  • The two of us, as the app's admins, can see the data when we need to fix a problem, review a report, or delete an account.

4Signing in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, Google gives us your name and email address (the email and profile permissions). We use them only to create and sign in to your Yalla account. We don't read your Gmail, contacts, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

Yalla's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6The AI features

The AI features (understanding a typed request, ranking places, predicting attendance) are not live in the app yet. The models are trained on simulated data and on searches stored as described above. If running them means sending your data to another service, we will update this page before that happens.

7How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data until you delete your account. An account that was never confirmed by email is removed automatically.

There's no delete button in the app yet. To delete your account, email us from the address you signed up with at yalla.app.lb@gmail.com. Within 30 days we will:

  1. Delete your account. This removes your profile, interests, free time, friendships, groups, plans you host, your invites, seats and votes, your notifications, your phone's push address, your searches, and your place submissions and reports.
  2. Delete your profile photo.
  3. Remove notifications in other people's accounts that refer to you.
  4. Reject any place you added that was still waiting for review.
  5. Email you to confirm it's done.

Please know: plans you hosted are cancelled for everyone. Places you added that were already approved stay on the map, no longer linked to you. Short-term logs kept by the services above expire on their own schedule, and Firebase keeps your phone's push address until you uninstall the app.

8Children

Yalla is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

9This website

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' internet addresses for security.

10Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. Questions: yalla.app.lb@gmail.com.
